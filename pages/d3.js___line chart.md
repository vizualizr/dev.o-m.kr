	- What is `d` attribute in SVG?
		- “In chapter 1, we discussed how the d attribute is made of a series of commands that dictate how to draw a shape.” ([Meeks, 2024, p. 128](zotero://select/library/items/VHTGXJRT)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/FGBNWKIT?page=154&annotation=SJ2A5EPU))
		- ```html
		  <svg id="svg_css_ex1" viewBox="0 0 100 100" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
		    <path
		      fill="none"
		      stroke="red"
		      d="M 10,30
		         A 20,20 0,0,1 50,30
		         A 20,20 0,0,1 90,30
		         Q 90,60 50,90
		         Q 10,60 10,30 z
		         " />
		  </svg>
		  ```
